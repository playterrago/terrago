Privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we store

Terrago has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Your game progress, streaks and best scores are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so you can pick up where you left off. That data stays on your device, and you can delete it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Cookies and ads

Terrago doesn't show ads and doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Fonts

Text is displayed with fonts loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of delivering them.
