Roadtripo

How to play

  1. Drive across the lower 48 from the start state to the finish state.
  2. On the way you want to see one famous landmark, so your route has to pass through the state it's in.
  3. Name the states you'd drive through, in any order. Each must share a border with the next.
  4. Every state you name joins your route. At the end you'll see the shortest possible route, so you can compare.
  5. You win once your states link start, landmark and finish. You get the shortest route plus 4 spare guesses.
