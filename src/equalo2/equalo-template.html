Equalo

How to play

  1. Every day: 5 to 10 countries and one statistic, like coastline or population.
  2. Drag every flag onto the left or right pan. Or tap a flag, then tap a pan.
  3. Press Weigh. The heavier side drops and you see how far apart the sides are. You get 3 weighs.
  4. There is always a split that balances exactly. Your score is 1,000, minus 10 points for every 1% your closest weigh was apart, minus 100 for every weigh after the first. So an exact balance is worth 1,000, 900 or 800.
