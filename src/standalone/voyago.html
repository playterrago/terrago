Voyago

How to play

  1. Ten geography prompts a day, like "Name a country that borders Brazil". You get one answer and 30 seconds for each.
  2. Valid answers earn points. Rarer answers score higher: from 10 points for the most obvious answer up to 100 for one almost nobody thinks of. Wrong, skipped or timed-out answers score 0. The best possible day is 1,000.
  3. Your points carry you around the world from Brussels to Kampala, 16 legs by car and plane. 1,000 points lands you in Kampala.
  4. Refreshing doesn't reset the clock, and each prompt takes one answer.

How rarity is scored

  1. For every prompt, all the valid answers are ranked from most to least well known, using a fixed familiarity score for each country (Terrago's own rating, not player data).
  2. An answer's position in that ranking sets its tier: top quarter Tourist 10, then Traveller 25, Explorer 45, Rare find 65, Deep cut 85, and the rarest 7% Off the map 100.
  3. The ranking is fixed before the day starts, so the same answer always earns the same points for every player, however many people play.
