Voyago

How to play

  1. Ten geography prompts a day, like "Name a country that borders Brazil". You get one answer and 30 seconds for each.
  2. Any correct answer scores, but the less obvious it is, the more it's worth: from 15 points for the first answer most people think of, up to 130 for a real deep cut.
  3. Your points carry you around the world from Brussels to Kampala, 16 legs by car and plane. Every leg is the same number of points.
  4. A perfect day, the rarest answer every time, lands you in Kampala.
