Roadtrip

How to play

  1. Drive from the start to the finish across the US, Canada and Mexico.
  2. Name the states and provinces you'd pass through, in any order. Each must share a land border with the next (bridges and river crossings count).
  3. 🟩 is on a shortest route, 🟧 is a small detour, 🟥 is a big detour.
  4. You win once your guesses link start to finish. You get the shortest route plus 4 spare guesses.
