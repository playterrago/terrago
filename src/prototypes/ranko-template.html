Ranko

How to play

  1. The same five countries appear in all five rounds.
  2. Each round asks you to rank them by a different stat. Drag the rows, or use the arrows.
  3. Lock it in to see the real values. A country in exactly the right spot is green; one spot off is yellow.
  4. 25 spots in total. New countries every midnight.
