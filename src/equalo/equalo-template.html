Equalo

How to play

  1. Seven countries, three rounds: population, area and economy (GDP).
  2. Put every country on the left or right pan so the two sides weigh the same.
  3. Each country's number stays hidden. Press Weigh to see the real totals on each pan and how far apart they are. You get 3 weighs per round, and your best one counts.
  4. Your score is how far off your best weigh is, as a share of the total. 🟩 within 2% of the best possible balance, 🟨 within 10%, 🟥 further off.
