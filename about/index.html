Small daily games about the world.

Terrago makes five short geography puzzles every day. Everyone gets the same ones, each takes a few minutes, and they work on any phone or laptop. No app, no sign-up.

The games

Capitalo

Find the hidden capital city in six turns.

  1. Each turn, choose: guess a capital, or take a clue.
  2. A guess tells you how far you are from the answer, which direction to head, and how close you are as a percentage.
  3. Clues unlock in order: continent, population, number of land neighbours, the shape of the capital's name, then the country.
  4. Your last turn has to be a guess. The fewer turns you use, the better.

Polygo

Name the country from its outline.

  1. The outline starts as just 2 edges: a single line showing how long the country is and which way it lies.
  2. Every wrong guess doubles the detail: 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, then 128 edges.
  3. There are no other hints. The shape is the only clue.
  4. You have seven guesses. Solve it with as few edges as you can.

Equalo

Balance seven countries on a scale.

  1. Put each country on the left or right pan.
  2. Three rounds with the same countries: population, area, then GDP.
  3. Each country's number stays hidden. Press Weigh to see the real total on each pan. You get three weighs per round.
  4. Your best weigh is scored against the best split possible: 🟩 within 2%, 🟨 within 10%, 🟥 further off.

RoadtripoBETA

Plan a drive across the lower 48 states.

  1. You get a start state, a finish state, and one famous landmark you want to see on the way.
  2. Name the states you'd drive through, in any order. Each must share a border with the next, and one of them has to hold the landmark.
  3. 🟩 is on a shortest route that includes the landmark, 🟧 is one step off, 🟥 is a big detour. 📍 marks the landmark's state.
  4. You get the shortest route plus four spare guesses.

VoyagoBETA

Rare answers take you further.

  1. Ten geography prompts a day, like "Name a country within two border crossings of Hungary". One answer and 30 seconds each.
  2. Any correct answer scores, but the less obvious it is, the more it's worth: from 15 points for the answer most people think of first, up to 130 for a real deep cut.
  3. Your points carry you around the world from Brussels to Kampala, 16 legs by car and plane. A perfect day gets you all the way.

How it works

  • New puzzles at midnight, your local time. Everyone in the world plays the same puzzles that day.
  • Streaks and stats are saved in your browser, so there's nothing to sign up for. They stay on the device you play on.
  • Share your result with the button at the end. It copies an emoji grid that shows how you did without spoiling the answer.
  • Practice rounds unlock once you've finished the day's puzzle, and never touch your streak.
